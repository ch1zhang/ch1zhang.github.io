I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Software System Security Assurance Group — WingTecher Lab](http://www.wingtecher.com/homeen) at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), supervised by Professor [Yu Jiang](https://sites.google.com/site/jiangyu198964/home). I received my Ph.D. in 2025 from the [Software Engineering Group](https://seg.nju.edu.cn/index.action) at Nanjing University, under the supervision of Professor [Linzhang Wang](https://cs.nju.edu.cn/lzwang/). In 2018, I earned dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Economics from [Sichuan University](https://en.scu.edu.cn/). From August 2022 to August 2023, I conducted research at the [Trustworthy Engineering of Software Technologies (TEST) Lab](https://nus-test.github.io/) at the National University of Singapore, supervised by [Manuel Rigger](https://www.manuelrigger.at/).

My research focuses on software bug detection, leveraging techniques such as fuzzing, static analysis, and machine learning. Recently, my work has centered on detecting logic and performance bugs in database management systems (DBMSs), where I have identified and confirmed over 400 bugs in widely-used DBMSs.
